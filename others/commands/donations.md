# /donations

Shows donations and donations received of clan members.

## Options

| Name | Type | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| `clan` | Text | Clan tag or name or alias. |
| `user` | User | Donation history of a linked user. |
| `season` | Text | The season to show donations for.<details><summary>View Options</summary>`2 Nov – 30 Nov 2026`, `5 Oct – 2 Nov 2026`, `7 Sep – 5 Oct 2026`, `10 Aug – 7 Sep 2026`, `13 Jul – 10 Aug 2026`, `15 Jun – 13 Jul 2026`, `18 May – 15 Jun 2026`, `20 Apr – 18 May 2026`, `23 Mar – 20 Apr 2026`, `23 Feb – 23 Mar 2026`, `26 Jan – 23 Feb 2026`, `29 Dec 2025 – 26 Jan 2026`, `1 Dec – 29 Dec 2025`, `3 Nov – 1 Dec 2025`, `6 Oct – 3 Nov 2025`, `25 Aug – 6 Oct 2025`, `28 Jul – 25 Aug 2025`, `30 Jun – 28 Jul 2025`</details> |
| `start_date` | Text | Starting date (YYYY-MM-DD) |
| `end_date` | Text | Ending date (YYYY-MM-DD) |
